## Other

- Swatch tiles in `assets/swatches/` are crops of the base colour maps of the
  scans above (flowerpot, Ugarit-type ingot, Terpsichore) and carry the same
  licences as their sources.
- Type: Playfair Display (SIL Open Font License 1.1), served by Google Fonts.
- Libraries: three.js r160 (MIT), GSAP 3.12.5 (GreenSock standard "no charge" licence).

Several marble and bronze files were converted (spec-gloss → metallic-roughness)
and their textures resized to 2048px; the geometry is unmodified. Originals as
downloaded are kept in `textures/`.
